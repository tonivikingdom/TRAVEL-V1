# P5E2 Batch 2 — Ground Transit Operational Changes

Status: Draft implementation on `feature/p5e2-ground-transit-operational-changes`; starting main `e4685c5f729e27b2f1a8cab65c8c76393a01178d`. It is not a main baseline until review, merge and main CI succeed.

Batch 1 established adopted service/corridor identity, ordered normalized observations, distinct `safety.boarding` and `safety.transferToNext`, execution state, risk reconciliation and opt-in monitoring. Batch 2 adds a separate `operational` assessment: whether the currently adopted service can still deliver its original boarding/alighting goal. It never merges boarding headway with onward transfer, and unknown transfer remains `UNKNOWN` rather than an invented five-minute minimum.

The normalized observation records boarding and alighting target serviceability (`SERVED`, `NOT_SERVED`, `UNKNOWN`), current terminus identity/label and operating scope. Missing fields in older observations become `UNKNOWN`. The versioned `ground-transit-operational-v1` domain policy classifies cancellation, early departure, material delay, platform changes, target loss/short-turn, terminus change and restoration; multiple change kinds can coexist. Materiality depends on service class/headway, leg duration, execution proximity and protected downstream consequences, rather than a single delay threshold. Identity-matched, fresh, ordered Provider facts need no GPS-style repeated-sample hysteresis. GPS inference retains its separate evidence policy.

`operational.disposition` is independent of `operational.requiredAction`. Cancellation or lost adopted stop makes the current plan no longer feasible and requests route reevaluation, but leaves AdoptedRoute and TransportEdge intact. A later trusted correction can restore the same active route if its original targets are explicitly served and there is no persisted ACTUAL miss. A completed leg is terminal; an ACTUAL departure earlier than the user's ACTUAL arrival at boarding cannot be reversed by an estimate. A replaced route cannot reenter the current plan. Normalized observation and state-transition history remain available.

Risk uses the existing ExecutionRisk model. Notifications use existing `NotificationEvent` presentation grouping by leg and accepted observation timestamp: related risk and operational changes share one active presentation, with stable replay dedupe and prior active warnings retired after recovery. `hasDownstreamImpact` is based only on risks causally associated with the affected leg, not arbitrary Trip risks. Provider unavailability remains `GROUND_TRANSIT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, never cancellation/no-route; monitoring generation fences still govern result commits and side effects.

Synthetic-only scenarios cover cancellation, short-turn, delay, platform, early departure and restoration. The Compose chain checks worker-accepted disruption, one STRONG presentation, unchanged formal route topology, later correction, resolved cancellation risk and retained observation history. No real or paid Ground Transit Provider, automatic Query/Preview/Adopt, Push, official UI, Staging or Production is implemented. F-05/F-06/F-07/F-08 remain open.
